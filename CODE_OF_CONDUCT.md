# Code of conduct

Participants should communicate respectfully, welcome good-faith questions, criticize work rather than people, respect privacy, and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and unwanted contact. Technical disagreement is welcome when grounded in evidence.

Do not share another person's private information or secrets in code, discussions or reports. Respect boundaries and stop unwanted behavior when asked.

No enforcement team or reporting address has been established. If a problem occurs, preserve a minimal private record and seek a verified project owner through a channel you already trust. Owners of a future community should publish a confidential reporting route and proportionate enforcement process before opening that community. No response timeline or enforcement commitment is implied by this local document.
